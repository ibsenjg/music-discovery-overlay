# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Music Discovery Overlay respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the observable impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's personal or account data.
- Keep feedback focused on the project and its work.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, deceptive claims, or unsafe instructions.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and label the report as a code-of-conduct concern. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, and other content that violates this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, with attention to the safety of the people involved.
